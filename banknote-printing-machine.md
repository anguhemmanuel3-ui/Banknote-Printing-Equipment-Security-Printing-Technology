## What Is a Banknote Printing Machine?

A banknote printing machine is specialized industrial printing equipment designed for controlled and authorized banknote-production environments.

The exact equipment configuration depends on the requirements of the authorized institution, production capacity, substrate, design specifications, quality standards, facility infrastructure, and applicable regulations.
